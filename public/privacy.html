Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 12, 2026

PopTracker ("we", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our data practices for the public static analytics platform.

1. Google AdSense & Cookies

We integrate Google AdSense to serve programmatic advertisements. Google uses cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

2. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate website usage, page performance, and traffic sources. Google may process limited device and usage signals according to its own privacy controls. You can manage Google analytics and advertising preferences through your Google account settings.

3. Third-Party Outbound Affiliate Links

This website contains contextual affiliate marketing referral links to global secondary marketplaces such as eBay. When you click on these links and complete transactions, we may earn a referral commission from the marketplace operators. We do not track, capture, or store any of your checkout or payment credentials.

4. Market Data Credentials

The public PopTracker website does not collect or store marketplace API keys. Production market data is generated from cached eBay completed-sales pages by a private local workflow; only cleaned static JSON files are published to the website.
