Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Data We Store Locally

PopTracker may store a user-provided SOLDCOMPS_API_KEY in the browser localStorage when the user chooses to connect a local market data stream. This key stays on the current device and is not committed to this repository by the application.

Market Data

PopTracker displays public completed-sales analytics, cached JSON market data, and demo data where clearly labeled. The site does not collect personal portfolio holdings.
